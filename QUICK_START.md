## 🔰 First Time Setup

### Step 1: Install the App on Your Phone

**On iPhone (iOS):**
1. Open Safari browser
2. Go to the app URL (your husband will share it)
3. Tap the **Share** button (box with arrow pointing up)
4. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Tap **"Add"** in top right

**On Android:**
1. Open Chrome browser
2. Go to the app URL
3. Tap the **Menu** (three dots)
4. Tap **"Install App"** or **"Add to Home Screen"**
5. Tap **"Install"**

✅ Now the app is on your home screen like any other app!

## 📚 How to Use the App

### Adding a New Client

1. From the home screen, tap **"Clients"**
2. Tap the **"Add Client"** button
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Client Name** (required) - e.g., "Priya Sharma"
   - **Weight** (optional) - in kg
   - **Height** (optional) - in cm
   - **Age** (optional)
   - **Blood Group** - Select from dropdown
   - **Goal** - e.g., "Weight Loss", "Weight Gain"
   - **Allergies** - Any food allergies
   - **Notes** - Any additional information
4. Tap **"Add Client"**

### Creating a Diet Plan

1. Go to **Clients**
2. Tap on the client's name
3. Select the day you want to plan for (Mon, Tue, Wed, etc.)
4. For each meal type:

   **Adding a Meal:**
   - Tap the **"+"** button on the meal card (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snacks)
   - Fill in the details:
     - **Time** - When to eat (e.g., "8:00 AM")
     - **Food Items** - List all foods (e.g., "Oats with milk\n1 banana\n5 almonds")
     - **Portions** - How much (e.g., "1 bowl", "2 pieces")
     - **Calories** - Total calories (optional)
     - **Notes** - Any special instructions
   - Tap **"Save Meal"**

   **Editing a Meal:**
   - Tap the **"Edit"** button on the meal card
   - Make changes
   - Tap **"Save Meal"**

   **Deleting a Meal:**
   - Tap the **trash icon** on the meal card
   - Confirm deletion

   **Copying Meal to Next Day:**
   - Tap the **copy icon** on the meal card
   - The meal will be copied to the same meal time tomorrow

5. Navigate between days using the **←** and **→** arrows
6. Repeat for all meals and days

### Exporting Diet Plan as PDF

1. Open the client's diet plan page
2. Tap the **"Export"** button (top right)
3. PDF will download automatically
4. Share via WhatsApp, Email, or any app

### Viewing Client Information

- Scroll down on the client's page to see:
  - Weight, Height, Age
  - Blood Group
  - Allergies
  - Goals
  - Notes

## 🔄 Data Management

**All data is saved on your device automatically!**

- **Clients** are saved as soon as you add them
- **Meals** are saved immediately when you tap "Save Meal"
- **No need to manually save** - everything is automatic

**Note:** If you reinstall the app or clear browser data, your diet plans will be deleted. Make sure to export important plans as PDFs!

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**App not opening?**
- Close and reopen the app
- If still not working, tell your husband

**Can't find a client?**
- Use the search box at the top of the Clients screen
- Check if you accidentally deleted them

**PDF not downloading?**
- Make sure you have internet connection
- Try again in a few seconds

**App looks different/small?**
- Make sure you installed it as an app (not just a bookmark)
- On iPhone: Use Safari, not Chrome

**Data disappeared?**
- Unfortunately, data is stored locally and can be lost if you clear browser data
- Always export important diet plans as PDFs
